## Decision vocabulary

- **Existing use:** preserve-existing-use (default during audit); escalate-to-owner (records a concern but does not authorize a change).
- **Proposed new use:** verified-compatible; permission-required; defer-new-use; link-to-original-only (only when no existing embed is being converted).

## Non-negotiable preservation rules

- This audit is not a removal, replacement or migration exercise.
- Never remove, replace, hide, disable or convert an existing embedded illustration to link-only as part of the audit, even when rights are unknown or appear restrictive.
- Unknown, unclear or conflicting rights are findings. Keep the existing asset in place, record the evidence and escalate potential legal/contractual concerns to the project owner.
- Any exceptional action affecting an existing asset requires a separate explicit instruction from the project owner.
- For a proposed new use, do not embed, copy, crop, adapt or redistribute until the exact asset and applicable permission are verified.
- A caption, alt-text string, thumbnail label or placeholder is not visual inspection of the actual asset.
- Attribution does not grant reuse rights. A repository-level licence does not automatically prove that every asset in the repository is covered.
- Do not recreate an unseen figure or claim a visual comparison that was not performed.
