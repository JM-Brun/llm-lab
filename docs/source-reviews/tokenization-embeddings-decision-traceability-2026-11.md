## Contributions deliberately not promoted to atomic rows in this pass

The collection dossier also uses Mikolov et al. for the historical distinction between static word-type vectors and Transformer token-lookup embeddings, and Ethayarajh (2019) for context dependence and anisotropy in the specific ELMo/BERT/GPT-2 models studied. Those roles and their limits are already summarized in the existing collection dossier and embedding/contextualization lessons. I did not fabricate extraction item IDs or page locators for them: their source-specific extraction records were not part of the four YAML records inspected for this bounded matrix. If a later content change depends on a claim unique to either source, retrieve its existing source audit/extraction first and add the exact item and locator then.

The same principle applies to other sources in the collection: an existing source reference or a broad synthesis paragraph is not enough to manufacture an atomic evidence row.

## Cross-cutting decision

The evidence supports the current architecture: keep the essential route concise; keep detailed tokenizer evaluation in the optional advanced bilingual lesson; use `text-data` and `tokenization-strategies` for pipeline/mechanism explanations; preserve the embedding/contextualization distinction in its existing pages. The sources do not justify a new concept ID, a new canonical route step, a second tokenizer implementation, a new metric register, or a new learner-facing page.

The only maintenance obligation exposed by this matrix is to preserve denominators, model/task scope, and the difference between descriptive association and causal intervention whenever the advanced lesson is edited.
