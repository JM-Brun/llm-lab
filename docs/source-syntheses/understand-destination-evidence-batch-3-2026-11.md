**Declared purpose:** trace representations to logits, explain what a Transformer block repeats, and distinguish network computation from generation of the next token.

## 2. Causal masking — `causal-masking` {#causal-masking}

**Page identity:** `site/fr/causal-masking.qmd` / `site/en/causal-masking.qmd`  
**Declared role:** explain which positions a causal attention operation may access and why this constraint is required for autoregressive prediction.

| Local contribution | Claim to preserve | Provenance | FR destination | EN destination | Presence and decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `causal-mask-ic-01-prefix-access-and-diagonal` | At query position (i), keys at (j \leq i) are allowed and keys at (j > i) are blocked. The diagonal is allowed because the input at position (i) contains (x_i), while its shifted training target is (x_{i+1}). | The decoder attention mask is described in **Vaswani et al. (2017)**. The precise indexing and shifted-target explanation are the project's explicit mathematical/pedagogical formulation. | [Definition](../../site/fr/causal-masking.qmd#causal-mask-definition) | [Definition](../../site/en/causal-masking.qmd#causal-mask-definition) | Explicit in both languages, including the inequality, additive mask and shifted-target explanation. Preserve the indexing convention; do not describe the diagonal as leakage of the target. |
| `causal-mask-ic-02-mask-before-softmax` | The additive mask assigns (0) to allowed scores and (-\infty) to blocked scores before softmax. Zeroing future weights after softmax without renormalizing changes the normalized distribution; zeroing and renormalizing is equivalent in exact arithmetic only when the remaining sum is nonzero. | **Project-derived mathematical explanation**; the cited Transformer paper supports decoder masking, while the equivalence qualification follows from normalization. | [Definition and score masking](../../site/fr/causal-masking.qmd#causal-mask-definition); [counterexamples](../../site/fr/causal-masking.qmd#causal-masking-counterexamples) | [Definition and score masking](../../site/en/causal-masking.qmd#causal-mask-definition); [counterexamples](../../site/en/causal-masking.qmd#causal-masking-counterexamples) | Explicit in both languages. Keep the exact-arithmetic/nonzero-sum qualification; do not present post-softmax zeroing as equivalent without renormalization. |
| `causal-mask-ic-03-mask-types-answer-different-questions` | Causal, padding, document-boundary and loss/target masks are not interchangeable: they constrain temporal visibility, padding visibility, cross-document interaction, and objective participation respectively. A loss mask alone does not block information flow through attention. | **Project-derived taxonomy** grounded in the distinct roles represented by the computational graph and training pipeline. The page delegates shifted-target/data preparation to the corpus lesson. | [Distinct mask roles](../../site/fr/causal-masking.qmd#causal-vs-other-masks) | [Distinct mask roles](../../site/en/causal-masking.qmd#causal-vs-other-masks) | Explicit in both languages. Preserve the distinction, especially attention visibility versus target inclusion in the loss. |
| `causal-mask-ic-04-prefix-invariance-is-a-model-property` | With fixed parameters and a fixed prefix, appending or changing future tokens must not change outputs already computed for that prefix in a causal model. Inspecting the triangular mask alone does not prove that the complete computation has no other future-information path. | **Project-derived testable consequence** of causal computation. The page points to the repository's full-GPT prefix-invariance regression test; this is targeted implementation evidence, not a formal proof for all architectures. | [Counterexamples and prefix invariance](../../site/fr/causal-masking.qmd#causal-masking-counterexamples); [Practice test](../../site/fr/exercises/causal-masking.qmd) | [Counterexamples and prefix invariance](../../site/en/causal-masking.qmd#causal-masking-counterexamples); [Practice test](../../site/en/exercises/causal-masking.qmd) | Explicit in both languages, with a corresponding dedicated exercise and a reference full-model regression test. Keep the assumptions (same model/parameters and fixed prefix) explicit. |
| `causal-mask-ic-05-causal-does-not-mean-previous-token-only` | Causal attention may use every earlier position, not merely the immediately preceding token, unless an additional restriction such as a sliding window is imposed. | **Project-derived clarification** of the inequality (j \leq i); no separate external source claim is made. | [Counterexamples](../../site/fr/causal-masking.qmd#causal-masking-counterexamples) | [Counterexamples](../../site/en/causal-masking.qmd#causal-masking-counterexamples) | Explicit in both languages. Retain as a short misconception correction, not a separate lesson. |

**Practice destination:** [FR](../../site/fr/exercises/causal-masking.qmd) / [EN](../../site/en/exercises/causal-masking.qmd). The dedicated questions test mask-before-softmax and prefix invariance; the shared attention exercises provide further computational practice. The page correctly links to the loss exercise for the separate target-mask question.

**Implementation/API qualification:** the supplementary note names the exact project implementation and its tests, and explicitly limits what they establish. The PyTorch 2.15 boolean-mask polarity and fully-masked-row behavior are API-specific conventions, not part of the mathematical definition of causal masking. Keep these details in the collapsed advanced resource and verify them against the installed API version when implementation changes.

**Existing-value assessment:** the lesson already contains more than the minimum useful contributions: the shifted-target explanation of the diagonal, the pre-softmax normalization argument, a typed distinction among masks, and a model-level prefix-invariance test. No new concept, route step or page is warranted.

## 3. Cross-page decisions and boundaries

- The Transformer overview owns the system-level path from input representations through blocks to logits. The Q/K/V and scaled-attention lessons own the detailed attention calculation; the causal-mask lesson owns the visibility constraint.
- The generation lesson owns prefill, iterative decode, selection policy and stopping. The Transformer overview only contrasts the parallel training pass with sequential free-running generation.
- The loss lesson owns target selection and reduction. The causal-mask lesson distinguishes loss masks from attention masks but does not duplicate loss construction.
- The reference pre-LN diagram is explicitly one common arrangement. Do not generalize its exact sublayer ordering or normalization choice to every Transformer architecture.
- The two bilingual pairs have matching conceptual claims and dedicated Practice companions. This source inspection does not certify rendered visual parity, mobile behavior, keyboard navigation, screen-reader behavior or learner comprehension.
