## 5. Cross-page decisions and route continuity

- The branch is not one undifferentiated chain: likelihood scores observed sequences at fixed parameters; loss turns target-level penalties into an objective; gradients differentiate that objective; the optimizer updates parameters; validation measures generalization without updating.
- The distinction between scoring and training is preserved even though both can condition on observed prefixes. Teacher forcing describes reference-token conditioning; likelihood scoring computes sequence probability at fixed parameters; training adds loss, gradients and updates.
- The loss mask selects target positions contributing to the objective. Attention masks govern information visibility. A loss-masked position can still affect another target's representation and receive an indirect gradient through attention.
- The French and English loss pages have a small heading-structure difference around the three-token numerical example: the French version uses a separate subheading; English includes the same values inline under the batch-loss explanation. The mathematical example is present in both; do not mistake this heading difference for missing content.
- The training-loop page uses an explicit anchor for the English replay example; the French counterpart relies on its generated heading ID. The conceptual example and its Practice destination are present in both. Rendered-anchor verification remains required.
- The FR/EN companions preserve the same task identities and core numerical content. No new concept, graph edge, route stage, page-map field, page or visual asset is required by this batch.
