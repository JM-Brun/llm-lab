This document verifies current Quarto source and exact destination anchors. Local contribution IDs are dossier-local aggregation keys; they are not canonical concepts, page-map fields or a new registry.

- Source items are named only when the corresponding extraction item exists. Project-derived reasoning is labelled explicitly.
- A source item supports only the claim and scope described in its row; it does not validate an entire page.
- Explicit anchors were read from source. Auto-generated heading IDs were checked against Pandoc's auto-identifier behavior.
- Practice destinations are recorded separately from core lessons. The existence of a worked exercise is not learner-outcome evidence.
- No page, concept, relation, computational graph, route or visual change is proposed by this audit.

## 5. Cross-page decisions

- The panorama's three-use model and the computational spine remain project-owned synthesis; the Lin Perspective adds a diagnostic lens but does not replace the canonical computational model.
- Text-data's exact GPT-2 IDs are reproducible project examples. Ahia's multilingual study is supporting evidence for scoped token-budget effects, not a universal tokenizer ranking.
- The positional insertion-point taxonomy is supported by a mix of primary mechanism papers and a secondary synthesis. The permutation-equivariance derivation and the computability/generalization distinction are project-derived.
- The contextualized-representation lookup invariant and causal past/future intervention are project-derived from the model computation. Ethayarajh supports the empirical contextuality/anisotropy discussion within the paper's tested scope.
- No new concept, relation, route step, PAGE-MAP field, page, or visual asset is required by this batch.
