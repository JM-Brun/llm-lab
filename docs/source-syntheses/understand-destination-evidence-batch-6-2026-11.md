## 3. Cross-page decisions and route continuity

- ICL and context engineering answer adjacent but distinct questions: how a model uses demonstrations already in its prompt, and how an application decides what to transmit. The route generation → in-context-learning → context-engineering is a pedagogical progression, not a sequence of internal Transformer operations.
- Context engineering's three levels connect application selection to tokenized input and causal visibility. The application cannot make a future position visible to an earlier causal query merely by storing it in the prompt.
- RAG can supply information to an ICL situation, but retrieval, prompt conditioning and parameter updates remain distinct operations.
- The contextualized-representations lesson owns the transformation of accessible token inputs into hidden states. Context engineering owns system-level selection and assembly; it must not duplicate the Transformer explanation.
- FR/EN page-map scopes, purposes, route links and dedicated Practice destinations match. The examples and terminology are localized without changing the conceptual boundary.
- No new concept, graph edge, route stage, page-map field, page or visual asset is required by this batch.
