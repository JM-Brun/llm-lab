# Licensing scope

The repository-wide CC BY-SA 4.0 grant applies only to original project-authored prose in Quarto pages and documentation. It does not apply to images, diagrams, illustrations, SVG files, screenshots, photographs, fonts, datasets, model weights, or other non-text assets.

In particular, all local SVG files under `site/images/` are excluded until their authorship, adaptation history, and rights have been confirmed and the visual asset register explicitly records the applicable licence. Externally hosted figures and other third-party materials remain subject to their respective rights holders' terms. Attribution and source links do not by themselves grant reuse permission.

Where a page or document contains third-party material, only the project-authored portions for which the project holds the necessary rights are covered by the project licence.

See [the visual asset register](visual-assets-register.md) for asset-level evidence and unresolved checks, and [LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md) for the formal licence terms.
