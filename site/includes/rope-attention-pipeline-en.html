From vectors to output: one attention head, step by step

A deterministic toy example with three tokens and one 2D head. Q, K and V projections are given; we isolate the effects of RoPE, causal masking and softmax.

1 · Projections

Q, K and V come from the same hidden-state stream through three distinct projections.

2 · Position

RoPE rotates Qᵢ and each Kⱼ according to position; V remains unchanged.

3 · Scores QKᵀ / √d

The dot product is computed for each candidate key.

4 · Causal mask

Future positions are excluded before softmax.

5 · Softmax

Allowed scores become weights that sum to 1.

6 · Aggregate V

The output is the weighted sum of accessible value vectors.

These numbers are a pedagogical toy example, not activations measured from a trained model. Turning off the causal mask intentionally demonstrates non-causal attention.
