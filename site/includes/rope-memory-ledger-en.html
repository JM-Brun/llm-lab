One-layer estimate: cos/sin tables = 2 × sequence × (rotary dimensions / 2) elements; KV cache = 2 × batch × sequence × KV heads × full head dimension. This assumes K and V remain full-width in the cache. It excludes allocator overhead, alignment, replication and other buffers. Cache quantization is represented only as an assumed bytes-per-element size, not as a specific format.
