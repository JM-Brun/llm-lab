Why pairing conventions matter

The two layouts below describe the same two rotations when coordinate order and frequency assignment stay consistent.

Interleaved: [x₀, y₀, x₁, y₁]

Pairs: (0, 1) at θ₀; (2, 3) at θ₁

Split-half: [x₀, x₁, y₀, y₁]

Pairs: (0, 2) at θ₀; (1, 3) at θ₁

The starting vector is [1, 2, 3, 4] in interleaved layout. The permutation to split-half is [1, 3, 2, 4]. The split-half result is converted back to interleaved order before comparison.
